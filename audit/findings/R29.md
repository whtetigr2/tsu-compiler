# R29 -- representation is the dominant lever, and the compiler only searches half of it

**Status:** MEASURED, open. Two gaps, both with evidence, neither fixed here.

## The observation

Four consecutive findings turned on representation rather than on a hardware
limit: R25, R26, R27 and R28. That is worth checking rather than admiring, so
this measures how much representation actually costs.

## How much does the encoding change the hardware cost?

Every shipped program, compiled under each encoding the compiler searches,
reporting spins and max degree:

    program                  domain_wall        one_hot      spins ratio
    ecology_lotka_lite         16sp  d4        32sp  d9          2.00x
    terrain_layout_6x6         72sp  d9       108sp d10          1.50x
    codon_opt_tiny             12sp  d4        18sp  d4          1.50x
    seq_design_longer          16sp  d4        24sp  d4          1.50x
    floorplan_4zone             8sp  d5        12sp  d5          1.50x
    ilt_mask_adjacency_2x2      8sp  d3        12sp  d4          1.50x
    ...and twelve more at exactly 1.00x

**Twelve of nineteen programs show no difference at all.** Those are the ones
whose variables are binary: one bit is one bit, and there is nothing to choose.
The choice exists only where variables are categorical, and there it costs up to
twice the die.

`ecology_lotka_lite` is the sharpest case, and the spin count is not the
interesting part: the encoding takes max degree from **4 to 9**. Degree is what
decides whether a model fits at all, so a representation choice can be the
difference between compiling and not.

## The gap: three levers, and the compiler searches one

    lever                        what it changes              searched?
    coefficient precision        bits per coefficient         regime.analyse_regime
    encoding                     domain_wall vs one_hot       SLICE_ENCODINGS, yes
    variable elimination         which variables exist        NO

There is no presolve pass. `passes/` holds analyse, encode, expressibility,
lower, place, program, route, search, split and verify. Nothing removes a
variable that cannot take a value.

That is unusual. Every serious mixed-integer or SAT solver runs presolve first,
because eliminating a variable is the cheapest possible win: it costs nothing
downstream and shrinks every pass that follows.

### Evidence A: folding needed an elimination found by hand

R28. The HP folding model failed Z1's degree gate at 35 against a cap of 16.
Deleting the (monomer, site) pairs that a bipartite lattice makes unreachable
took it to degree 15, and it placed. That pruning is exact, and a test proves it
discards none of the 2,172 real folds.

Nothing in the compiler looks for that. It was found by thinking about the
problem, which is precisely the work a presolve pass exists to do instead.

### Evidence B: a workload with two reachable states

`diversity_reachable` across every receipt small enough to enumerate:

    prog_market_binary_factors      1024
    prog_maxcut_cycle7               128
    prog_sat_3tiny                   128
    prog_codon_opt_tiny              729
    prog_floorplan_4zone              81
    prog_number_partition             64
    prog_ilt_mask_adjacency_2x2       35
    prog_knapsack_tiny                32
    prog_jobshop_tiny                 22
    prog_roster_shift_conflicts        7
    prog_ecology_lotka_lite            2      <--

`ecology_lotka_lite`'s contract forbids the two species sharing an edge. On a
connected 4x4 grid that forces every cell to the same species, so exactly **2 of
65,536** assignments validate, enumerated directly.

**The compiler measured this correctly.** `verification.json` records
`diversity_reachable: 2` and `diversity_distinct: 2`. Credit where it is due:
nothing here is hidden or wrong.

What is missing is that nothing READS it. The shelf presents this program the
same way it presents `codon_opt_tiny`, which has 729 reachable states, and it is
one of the three entries carrying a VERIFIED badge. The badge means the workload
compiles. It does not mean the workload is a meaningful thing to sample, and
these are not the same claim.

The program's own description also says same-type neighbours clump into
"patches", plural. With two uniform states available there is exactly one patch,
always. The description and the contract disagree, and the receipt sides with the
contract.

A presolve pass would have collapsed this to a constant at compile time and said
so, instead of leaving it to a verification statistic no interface displays.

## What to do, cheapest first

1. **Surface `diversity_reachable` on the shelf.** The number already exists in
   every receipt. A program with two reachable states should not look identical
   to one with seven hundred. This costs nothing but wiring.
2. **Decide what VERIFIED means.** Today it means an oracle test compiles the
   workload. It could also require the state space to be non-trivial, which
   `ecology_lotka_lite` would currently fail.
3. **Add a presolve pass** that propagates the contract's declared rules before
   encoding, fixes variables the rules force, and reports what it removed. The
   contract layer already holds hard constraints in a machine-readable form
   (`forbid_both`, `forbid_value_with`, `forbid_value_pair_over_edges`), so the
   input a presolve pass needs is already there.

## Scope

Nineteen programs, two encodings, one target. The 2x figure is the largest
measured here and is not a bound. Nothing about energy.
